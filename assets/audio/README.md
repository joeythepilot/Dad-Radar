## Experimental primary-display power contacts (October 10, 2026)

- `power-relay.wav`: excerpt of **A relay switch being activated.**, bassmosphere, Freesound 384698, https://freesound.org/people/bassmosphere/sounds/384698/ . The author describes a 1970s pinball-machine relay recorded with a Tascam DR-40. CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . Source bytes: public HQ MP3 preview https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/384/384698_1067129-hq.mp3 (not the login-required original WAV). Excerpt starts 0.040 s, length 0.149 s.
- `power-toggle.wav`: excerpt of **Push Toggle Switch 1**, cookies+policy, Freesound 556630, https://freesound.org/people/cookies%2Bpolicy/sounds/556630/ . The author identifies a mechanical latching toggle switch; CC0 1.0. Source bytes: public HQ MP3 preview https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/556/556630_10713160-hq.mp3 . Excerpt starts 0.860 s, length 0.320 s, selecting one actuation from the take.

Both converted to 44.1 kHz mono PCM16 WAV, gain ×0.65 before PCM quantization, with 2 ms input/10 ms output fades to prevent edit-boundary pops. No pitch shifting, generated noise, oscillator resonance, time stretching or synthetic contact fallback. Relay sample is reused quietly as a generic clock/weekly mechanical detent; it is not a recording of DadRadar’s actual drum/wheel hardware. Toggle sample follows deliberate power command. Supplemental synthesized transformer hum is also removed. Existing approved split-flap recording/cue and all normal/mobile audio behavior remain unchanged. These assets are experimental, not family-approved or deployed.
