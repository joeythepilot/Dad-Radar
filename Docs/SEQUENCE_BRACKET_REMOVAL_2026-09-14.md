# Remove the rejected sequence-counter bracket

Scope: family beta, agent/mobile-companion.

Remove only the oversized CSS brass bracket below Active Sequence, including
its responsive overrides. No replacement support, new artwork, or rod is added
for the counter. Its position, dimensions, text, data, and housing stay unchanged.
The two clock housings and their supplied-artwork brass rods are unchanged.
Map transport and sound logic are unchanged.

The future counter placement/mount is deferred, not visually approved by this
change. The browser proof now requires both counter attachment pseudo-elements
to be absent while retaining the clock-rod, layout, and transport assertions.
The sequence stylesheet cache version advances to v6.

No production packaging or home-server installation is performed.
