# Dad Radar Product Vision

Dad Radar is not a flight tracker.

Dad Radar is not a Raspberry Pi project.

Dad Radar is not a smart display.

Dad Radar is an heirloom.

Its purpose is to help an airline pilot's family remain emotionally connected throughout every trip.

It should feel like a handcrafted piece of airline operations equipment from the late 1950s or early 1960s.

Every decision should reinforce five principles:

1. Calm
2. Timeless
3. Honest
4. Functional
5. Beautiful

Technology should disappear.

The story should remain.
